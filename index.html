What we are building toward.

These are our goals for every device we make. We will share the details, and the proof, as we get closer to launch.

Made to last

Parts you can repair and upgrade, and batteries designed to keep going for years, so you replace a device less often.

Clean from start to end

Recycled materials, cleaner manufacturing, and a take-back scheme so old devices are reused or recycled instead of thrown away.

Light on energy

Efficient hardware and software that do the same work with less power, and charging that works well with clean electricity.
