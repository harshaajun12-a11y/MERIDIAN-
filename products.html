Our concept devices.

A phone and a laptop designed to be repaired, reused and kept for years. First sales are planned for 2030.

Meridian Phone

A phone you can open, understand and keep. We are designing it around the idea that the best phone is the one you do not need to replace.

  • Repair-first design: the battery and screen are meant to be swapped without special tools.
  • A battery built for years of daily use.
  • Recycled materials wherever we can use them.
  • An efficient chip and software that sip power.

Design goals. Concept render, not final.

Meridian Laptop

A laptop made from parts you can replace one at a time, so one worn-out piece never means a whole new machine.

  • Modular parts for the battery, storage and keyboard.
  • A long-life battery and efficient hardware.
  • A take-back scheme so old laptops are reused or recycled.
  • Software built to run well on older hardware.

Design goals. Concept render, not final.

Made the same way, every time.

Materials

Recycled metals and plastics where they meet our quality bar, and a plan to publish what each device is made from.

Batteries

Cells chosen for long life and safety, in packs that can be replaced instead of throwing the device away.

Software

Updates for as long as we can, and software that does the job with less energy.
