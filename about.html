Why Meridian.

A meridian is a line from pole to pole that everyone can navigate by. We want to be a reference point for technology that is kinder to the planet.

What we believe.

Phones and laptops are some of the most wasteful things we buy. They are thrown away while still working, and most are hard to repair. We think that can change.

  • Make it last. A device you keep for years is the cleanest device.
  • Keep it clean. From materials to recycling, we want to waste less at every step.
  • Aim high. Space is our long-term goal, and it keeps our engineering honest.

Our plan for the next four years.

Plans and dates are goals and may change.

  1. 2026Research, brand and first designs.
  2. 2027Design work and early prototypes.
